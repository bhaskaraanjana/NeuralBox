NeuralBox Enterprise Data Policy
Version: 2026-02-14

Scope
This policy applies only to Enterprise contract customers.

Retention and Compliance
- Enterprise audit logs are retained for 90 days
- Backup snapshots are retained for 35 days
- Compliance contact alias: compliance-enterprise@neuralbox.local

Access Controls
- All production access requires MFA
- Privileged access sessions are recorded
- Security review frequency: quarterly

Data Regions
- Enterprise default region: Canada East
- Optional region pinning is available for approved contracts

Operational Notes

- Dedicated escalation identifier for enterprise reviews: ORBIT-990

Unique reference phrase
